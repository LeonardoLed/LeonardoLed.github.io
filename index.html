Leonardo Ledesma-Domínguez

Bioinformatics scientist, engineer and research professor

My research focuses on bioinformatics and artificial intelligence, with particular emphasis on designing deep learning algorithms for prediction, classification, and algorithm enhancement in genetics and omics.

As an engineer, I have more than 14 years of experience managing computing systems, databases, data engineering initiatives, and institutional technology projects.

I am equally passionate about teaching, mentoring, and sharing knowledge with the next generation of engineers and scientists.
